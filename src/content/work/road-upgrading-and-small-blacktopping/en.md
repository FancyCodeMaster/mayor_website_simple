The ward has undertaken road upgrading and small blacktopping works as part of its physical infrastructure development efforts. The initiative covers approximately 40 kilometers of roads within the ward.

The work is focused on improving existing road conditions and making local transportation routes more accessible and convenient for residents. Road improvement is an important part of strengthening connectivity between communities and supporting everyday movement within the ward.

Through the upgrading and blacktopping of local roads, the ward aims to improve the overall quality and usability of its road infrastructure while supporting better access for residents and local activities.

The ward provides a 50% subsidy for livestock farming through cooperatives.

The program is designed to provide financial support to people involved in livestock farming and help reduce the cost burden associated with livestock-related activities.

By delivering the subsidy through cooperatives, the program supports a community-based approach to livestock farming assistance. The initiative falls under the ward's economic development activities and is intended to support local livelihood opportunities.

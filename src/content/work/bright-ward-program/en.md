The Bright Ward Program focuses on improving local street lighting through the installation of 1,200 street lights.

The program is intended to improve lighting along local roads and public areas, making movement and visibility more convenient for residents. Better street lighting can also contribute to a more accessible and usable public environment during evening and nighttime hours.

The installation of 1,200 street lights represents a significant local infrastructure initiative aimed at expanding lighting coverage across the ward.

The ward provides free technical support for vegetable farming as part of its economic development initiatives.

The program is intended to assist residents engaged in vegetable cultivation by providing access to technical guidance and support. Such assistance can help farmers make better-informed decisions regarding their farming activities.

The initiative represents an effort to strengthen local agricultural activities by making technical knowledge and support more accessible to people involved in vegetable farming.

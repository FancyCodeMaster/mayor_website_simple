The ward has carried out work to open and develop approximately 12 kilometers of internal roads as part of its local infrastructure development program.

The initiative is intended to improve access within residential and community areas by establishing or opening internal road routes. Better internal connectivity can make everyday travel easier for residents and improve access to different parts of the ward.

The development of internal roads forms an important part of the ward's broader effort to strengthen local transportation infrastructure and improve connectivity at the community level.

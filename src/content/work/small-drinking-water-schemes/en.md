Six small drinking water schemes are in operation within the ward, providing residents with improved and more convenient access to drinking water.

These schemes form part of the ward's efforts to improve basic water infrastructure and support reliable access to drinking water at the local level.

By keeping small-scale drinking water schemes in operation, the ward is working to bring essential water services closer to communities and make access to drinking water more convenient for citizens.
